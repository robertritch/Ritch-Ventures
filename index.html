Founder Bottleneck

Inconsistent Growth

Start with clarity. Continue with implementation.

Every relationship begins with a defined operating problem—not an open-ended consulting arrangement.

Every engagement starts with a diagnostic look at your business strategy, revenue model, sales process, organization, and execution. From there, we customize an operating plan and partnership model to your specific constraints.

Business Operating Assessment

A focused review of the company's economics, revenue process, organization, systems, priorities, and execution. The result is a written 100-day operating plan.

  • Business model and revenue
  • Pricing and margins
  • Sales and customer acquisition
  • Cash flow and management reporting
  • Organization and accountability
  • Technology and operating systems
  • Immediate growth constraints
  • Priority actions for the next 100 days

90-Day Operating Partnership

Ritch Ventures works alongside ownership to implement the plan, establish an operating cadence, and move the highest-impact initiatives from discussion to execution.

  • Weekly executive operating meeting
  • Company scorecard and priorities
  • Revenue and pipeline oversight
  • Financial and operating visibility
  • Process and technology implementation
  • Vendor and partner coordination
  • Organizational accountability
  • Monthly performance review

Shared Growth Systems

Selected clients can access coordinated support for websites, CRM, automation, lead flow, media, technology, and specialist providers—without having to assemble every resource independently.

  • Website and conversion infrastructure
  • CRM and workflow automation
  • Lead capture and follow-up
  • Sales-pipeline systems
  • Provider selection and coordination
  • Content and publishing support
  • Technology implementation
  • Management reporting

Beyond these core offerings, we work on business strategy, organizational structure, sales and marketing systems, process improvement, and partnership development — customized to each company's constraints.

A practical path from constraint to operating discipline.

We focus on a small number of measurable priorities, establish clear ownership, and build systems that can continue without permanent dependence on outside help.

1

Fit Conversation

Determine whether the company, owner, and operating problem are a strong match.

2

Operating Assessment

Identify the constraints with the greatest economic and organizational impact.

3

100-Day Plan

Define priorities, owners, milestones, operating metrics, and the implementation sequence.

4

Implementation

Install the systems, cadence, and accountability required to create durable progress.

Best suited for owners with a real business and a solvable operating constraint.

Strong Fit

  • A privately held, founder-led company
  • Existing customers and operating revenue
  • An owner with decision-making authority
  • A clear need for stronger systems or execution
  • A viable underlying product or service
  • Willingness to share operating and financial information
  • Readiness to implement—not only discuss—change

Not the Right Engagement

  • A pre-revenue idea seeking unpaid development
  • A company unable to fund operating work
  • An owner unwilling to measure performance
  • A business dependent on an immediate capital raise to survive
  • A request for investment recommendations
  • A request to solicit investors or sell securities
  • A project dependent on unsupported claims or guarantees

Robert Ritch focuses on the business of running a business.

Robert Ritch is the founder of Ritch Ventures. He works with founders and business owners on operating strategy, revenue systems, organizational structure, technology, business development, and execution.

Ritch Ventures is industry-agnostic by design. The underlying vocation may change, but the disciplines required to build a healthy company—clear economics, accountable leadership, repeatable sales, useful reporting, and consistent execution—remain transferable.

Robert's role is to help build and operate the company around the underlying product, service, or expertise.
